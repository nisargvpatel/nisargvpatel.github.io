I am Nisarg Patel [નિસર્ગ પટેલ], and I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Comparative Media and Culture program at USC (joined 2021). A South Asianist by training, I am currently working on a Dissertation Project titled “Enframed: Visual Culture(s) and the Changing Perceptions of History in India (1860-1960).” My dissertation project is supervised by [Prof. Neetu Khanna](https://dornsife.usc.edu/profile/neetu-khanna/) and advised by [Prof. Priya Jaikumar](https://cinema.usc.edu/directories/profile.cfm?id=6548), [Prof. Vanessa Schwartz](https://dornsife.usc.edu/vanessaschwartz/), [Prof. Panivong Norindr](https://dornsife.usc.edu/profile/panivong-norindr/) and [Prof. Ronald Mendoza-de Jesús](https://complit.emory.edu/people/bios/mendozadejesus-ronald.html) 
In my project, I examine 19th-century colonial stereographs, 20th-century Indian cinema, and post-colonial vernacular literature, in order to trace the shifts in ‘perception of history’ and, thereby, construct a ‘history of perception.’
