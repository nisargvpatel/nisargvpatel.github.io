## Course Description

This graduate seminar examines the relationship between history and narration in modern South Asia, exploring how literary and theoretical texts shape our understanding of the past, present, and future. Through interdisciplinary inquiry, the course investigates key debates on colonialism, memory, caste, subalternity, violence, and the archive, treating literature as a form of historical knowledge and history as an ongoing field of narrative contestation.
