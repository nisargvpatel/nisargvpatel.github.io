## Course Description

An introductory exploration of modern Indian literature from the colonial to the contemporary period, examining how literary texts engage with key debates on colonialism, nationalism, identity, modernity, and globalization. Through close reading and critical theory, the course develops analytical skills to understand literature as a historically and politically mediated form of aesthetic expression.
